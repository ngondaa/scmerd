Getting there

Plan your arrival to the 2026 conference

The event will take place at the University of the Witwatersrand's West Campus in Johannesburg, with the main conference day starting at 09h00.

Venue details

  • Building: Southwest Engineering Building (SWEB)
  • Campus: West Campus, University of the Witwatersrand
  • Address: 1 Jan Smuts Avenue, Braamfontein, Johannesburg, 2000
  • Conference start: 09h00, Friday 09 October 2026

Travel note

Use main access routes to West Campus and allow extra time for parking and check-in on arrival. The gala dinner venue will be communicated closer to the conference date.
